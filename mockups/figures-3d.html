3D figures for the home page

Each figure is a small live 3D scene (Three.js), shown in two renderings. Clay is soft-lit with rounded edges and real shadows, and turns a little towards your pointer. Flat iso keeps the straight isometric angle and three-shade look of the current figures, so it sits closer to what A4 has now. Both run in the module colour. Hover a figure for a PNG of it, which is also how we could ship them as still images.

Module 1 · Fundamentals of AI, option AFlat CNN

Laid out side on like the textbook diagram: the chest film on the left, then three sets of feature maps that get smaller, then two answers on the right. It shows the one idea behind image AI, that the model looks at a small patch at a time.

Moves: a small window slides across the film and each patch becomes one cell on the next map, with lines joining them. When it finishes, "Pneumonia" lights up.

Module 1 · Fundamentals of AI, option BLearns from labelled films

No network at all. Films with Normal and Pneumonia labels go into a block marked as the model, and then the model puts a label on a new film. That is the whole of "supervised learning" in one picture.

Moves: the labelled films drop into the model one by one and its lights fill in, then the new film on the right gets "Pneumonia 0.91".

Clay

Module 1 · Fundamentals of AI, option CSimple network

Nearly flat: the chest film on the left, then three columns of dots and an answer pair, all joined by lines. The lines stand for the weights. Orange lines push towards an answer and blue ones push away, and thicker means stronger.

Moves: pulses run from the film through each layer to the answers, while the lines slowly brighten, fade and change colour as the weights are trained. "Pneumonia" gets surer over a few rounds.
