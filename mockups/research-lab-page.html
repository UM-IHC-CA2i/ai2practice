Research Lab · independent track

Data science for clinical AI research

Six hands-on labs, from your first line of Python to a project of your own. Each one starts with a real clinical question. You work in Google Colab (free notebooks that run in your browser) and save to GitHub (a site that keeps every version of your code), so your progress becomes a portfolio.

The labs

One path, six stops

Work through them in order. Each lab opens with a short warm-up; if it's easy, you can skip ahead. Lab 6, your own project, is optional.

How you'll work with code

Less typing from scratch, more checking

Every notebook task is one of six kinds. Early labs build fluency by having you write code yourself. Later labs work the way research code is written now: you reuse your own earlier functions, have an AI draft code, and check it.

Where do I start?
