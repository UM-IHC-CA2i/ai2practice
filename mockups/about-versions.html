Clinical AI education built for practice.

AI2Practice is a free, open course that connects the foundations of artificial intelligence with clinical evaluation, implementation, research and teaching in radiology.

An initiative of the AAR / RRA Committee on Data Science & AI

Co-leads

AI2Practice is co-led by Shinjer Li and Florence Doo, with contributors from radiology, imaging informatics, AI, education and clinical practice.
