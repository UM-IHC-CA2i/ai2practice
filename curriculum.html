Core curriculum

The whole course at a glance

Three modules of short lessons, then an assessment. Each lesson introduces and explains key concepts as needed, allowing learners to progress independently without prior AI experience.

  • 3 modules
  • 14 core lessons
  • 15-20 min per lesson
  • One depth for everyone
