Learning depth

Choose a pathway

Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced describe learning depth, not professional title. AI2Practice provides a default pathway; educators and program directors may assign a different lesson/case mix for their learners.

The current core lessons have two content-depth variants: Concise / foundational and Deeper radiology. Beginner defaults to the concise version; Intermediate and Advanced default to the deeper version. Your role does not determine which version you may take.
