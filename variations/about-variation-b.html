Our Mission

This educational resource is being developed by members of the Committee on Data Science & AI of the Association of Academic Radiology (AAR) and the Radiology Research Alliance (RRA). Our goal is to help radiology trainees and educators navigate the risks that AI poses to clinical skill development.
