About AI2Practice

Clinical AI education built for practice.

AI2Practice is an open educational resource that connects the foundations of artificial intelligence with clinical evaluation, implementation, research, and teaching in radiology.

An initiative of the AAR / RRA Committee on Data Science & AI

Co-leads

AI2Practice is co-led by Shinjer Li and Florence Doo, with contributors spanning radiology, imaging informatics, AI, education, and clinical implementation.
