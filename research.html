AI Research Track

A hands-on path for building AI research skills in radiology

What Is the Research Track?

The research track is an optional extension of the clinical AI curriculum. It is designed for medical students and radiology residents who want to go beyond understanding AI as a clinical tool and learn how to contribute to AI research in radiology.

Across five modules, learners walk through setting up a development environment, working with research data, building and evaluating AI models, and designing their own research project. Each module is self-paced and builds on the last.

The Five Modules

1

How Do I Set Up the Tools I Need to Do AI Research?

GitHub, Python, command line, and Jupyter notebooks

2

How Do I Work with and Understand Research Data?

Data cleaning, visualization, statistics, bias, leakage, and train/test splits

3

How Do I Build and Run My Own AI?

Basic ML, neural networks, classification, segmentation, NLP, LLMs, and generative AI

4

How Do I Know Whether an AI Actually Works?

Evaluation metrics, error analysis, bias, calibration, validation, and generalizability

5

How Do I Design My Own AI Research Project?

Research questions, datasets, hypotheses, experiments, analysis, interpretation, and GitHub

Get Involved

If you are interested in contributing to AI research in radiology, we encourage you to complete the research track modules and then reach out to us with your project ideas. We are looking for:

  • Medical students and residents interested in AI-focused research projects
  • Completed module exercises or project proposals
  • Collaborators with clinical questions that could benefit from AI methods

To submit a project idea or get in touch, please contact us at ai-ready@radiology.edu.
