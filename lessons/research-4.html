R4

Advanced Image AI and NLP

CNNs, transfer learning, attention maps, radiology report NLP, and LLMs

Overview

This module goes deeper into AI architectures. You will work with convolutional neural networks (CNNs), explore transfer learning and attention maps to understand what an AI "sees," and apply natural language processing (NLP) to radiology reports. By the end, you will have experience with both image and text AI tools.

Content for this research module goes here.

  • Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image classification
  • Transfer learning: using pre-trained models
  • Attention / saliency maps: what does the AI focus on?
  • NLP on radiology reports: tokenization, keyword extraction
  • Text classification with scikit-learn or Hugging Face
  • Comparing AI-generated vs. human-written reports
  • Introduction to large language models (LLMs)
