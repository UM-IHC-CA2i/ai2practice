How Can I Use AI in Radiology?

Following an imaging study from order to follow-up — and where AI fits in

The Radiology Workflow

Most people think of radiology as "reading images," but that is only one piece of a much larger process. Every imaging study follows a path from the moment a clinician decides a patient needs a scan to the moment the results change that patient's care. AI tools are being used at every stage of this path.

Click through the steps below to see how each one works and where AI is making an impact.

Key Takeaways for Medical Students

As a medical student, you interact with this workflow every time you order imaging on the wards or review a radiology report. Knowing how the pipeline works helps you order the right study, understand what the radiologist's report is telling you, and recognize when you need to pick up the phone and call radiology directly.

Think About It

When you look at the workflow above, which steps do you interact with directly as a medical student? Which ones happen behind the scenes? Understanding this helps you see where communication breakdowns can occur — and where AI might help or hinder your learning.

Key Takeaways for Radiology Residents

As a radiology resident, you are directly involved in protocoling, interpretation, reporting, and communicating results. AI tools are already embedded in many of these steps at academic medical centers. Understanding where AI sits in your daily workflow — and what skills it might be quietly substituting for — is essential for your development.

Think About It

Consider the interpretation step: if an AI tool flags every pneumothorax on your chest CT worklist, are you getting enough practice detecting them yourself? How would you know if your pattern recognition for this finding was never fully developed?
