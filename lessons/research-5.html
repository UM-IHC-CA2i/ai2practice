Mini Research Project

Formulate a question, design an analysis, execute it, and write a structured abstract

Overview

This final module brings everything together. You will design and execute a mini research project from scratch — picking a question, analyzing data with the tools from R1–R4, and writing a structured abstract suitable for submission to a journal like Academic Radiology.

Content for this research module goes here.

  • Choosing a research question from provided options (or propose your own)
  • Designing your analysis plan
  • Executing the analysis using skills from R1–R4
  • Writing a structured abstract (Background / Methods / Results / Conclusion)
  • Downloading your completed notebook and uploading it to your GitHub fork
  • Submitting a pull request with all completed modules for instructor review
