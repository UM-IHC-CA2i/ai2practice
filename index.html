How the learning depths will differ

One depth is published today. All three choices currently share the same lessons and assessment. Separate versions are planned, and your saved choice will carry over when they are released.

BeginnerMedical students and anyone new to clinical AI

Core concepts and vocabulary, reading AI output on a study, and knowing when to ask for help. Guided cases with step-by-step feedback.

IntermediateResidents, fellows and clinicians who use AI

Interpreting performance metrics and evidence for real tools, choosing reading workflows, and handling AI disagreement. Mixed-difficulty cases.

AdvancedAttendings, AI leads and educators

Critical appraisal, local validation, monitoring and teaching others. The hardest cases, with subtle failure modes and dataset shift.
