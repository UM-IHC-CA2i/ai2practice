For Educators

Adapt AI2Practice into courses, conferences, residency teaching, journal clubs, and faculty development without rebuilding the conceptual framework from scratch.

Teaching approachCurriculum mapsTeaching resources

Teach the structure, not just the vocabulary

For Educators packages AI2Practice content into teachable sequences. The emphasis is on clinical reasoning: what learners need to understand, what decisions they should be able to make, and how to assess that understanding.
