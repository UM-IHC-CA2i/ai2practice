For Educators

Bring AI2Practice into your program.

Use the existing curriculum as a complete teaching sequence, or pull out individual lessons, cases, assessments, and optional extensions for a rotation, conference, journal club, or faculty-development program.

See the whole learning architecture at once.

The clinical curriculum is a shared three-module core with different depth for medical students and residents/fellows. Cases and assessment follow the core; Research Lab, practice leadership, and Looking Ahead remain optional extensions.
