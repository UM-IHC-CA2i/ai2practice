Research Lab

Research Lab

A parallel space for methods, study designs, emerging architectures, and open questions in clinical AI reliability, human–AI systems, monitoring, and advanced evaluation.

OverviewResearch themesResearch workbench

A parallel track for questions that are not yet settled

Research Lab is intentionally separate from the core learning progression. It is a place for methods, hypotheses, datasets, study designs, and emerging architectures that can inform future clinical AI practice.

Research themes

Theme 01

Reliability and heterogeneity

Measure where performance varies across sites, populations, protocols, devices, and time—and how to make those differences visible.

Theme 02

Human–AI systems

Study reliance, calibration of trust, task allocation, interface effects, and the conditions under which assistance helps or harms.

Theme 03

Post-market surveillance

Develop observability, monitoring, and anomaly-detection methods for deployed clinical AI systems.

Theme 04

Emerging architectures

Explore multimodal reasoning systems, agents, predictive models, digital twins, synthetic data, and in-silico evaluation while keeping clinical validation requirements explicit.

Research workbench

Use this area for paper concepts, protocol sketches, measurement frameworks, reproducible methods, and curated reading lists. Mature findings can feed back into the Foundations, Clinical Practice, and Adoption & Governance curriculum.
