Hands-on curriculum

Five modules from setup to an independent project.

The original Research Track remains the backbone of Research Lab. The sequence is cumulative: learners set up a reproducible environment, work with data, build models, explore advanced methods, and finish by designing a small project.

Current build: Modules 1–3 include substantive exercises and notebook-supported work. Modules 4–5 are already part of the curriculum structure but remain under active development.

Emerging directions · not part of the five-module core

Where Research Lab can grow next.

These themes are better treated as future research directions than as the first thing a learner sees. They can eventually host reading lists, study designs, methods notes, or project ideas once that content exists.

Reliability & heterogeneity

Site, population, protocol, device, and temporal variation in clinical AI performance.

Human–AI systems

Reliance, calibration of trust, workflow effects, task allocation, and interface design.

Monitoring & observability

Post-deployment measurement, drift, anomaly detection, and safety surveillance.

Emerging architectures

Multimodal systems, agents, predictive models, digital twins, and synthetic or in-silico evaluation.
