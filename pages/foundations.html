Foundations

Understand AI before you are asked to trust, evaluate, or deploy it. This section builds the core concepts needed to reason about clinical AI without requiring a technical background.

OverviewCore modulesInteractive curriculumWhere this leads

Build the mental model first

Foundations is the conceptual base for the rest of AI2Practice. The goal is not to turn clinicians into machine-learning engineers; it is to make the mechanics, metrics, and limitations of clinical AI legible enough to support good decisions later.

Core question: What does a clinician need to understand about an AI system before interpreting its evidence or using its output?

Core modules

Module 01

How clinical AI learns

Training data, labels, supervised and self-supervised learning, foundation models, and the difference between development and deployment.

Module 02

Performance beyond accuracy

Discrimination, calibration, thresholds, prevalence, uncertainty, and why a single summary metric rarely answers a clinical question.

Module 03

Generalization and shift

Population, site, scanner, protocol, workflow, and temporal differences that can separate reported performance from local performance.

Module 04

Failure modes and bias

Shortcut learning, subgroup performance, data leakage, confounding, and the practical meaning of robustness.
