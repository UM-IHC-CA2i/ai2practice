Question 1 of 15
An R2 has used AI nodule detection on every chest CT for 12 months. When moonlighting without AI, she misses a 9mm spiculated nodule she has never independently searched for. This is:
Never-skilling. The resident never built independent nodule search patterns. AI did the searching from day one.
Question 2 of 15
An attending mammographer has read independently for 15 years. After 2 years of AI-assisted CAD, her unassisted accuracy has dropped. This is:
Deskilling. The attending had well-established skills that eroded through disuse. The cognitive architecture exists but has weakened.
Question 3 of 15
After 6 months with an AI tool that overcalls consolidation on poorly inspired films, a resident starts overcalling consolidation on well-inspired films without AI. This is:
Mis-skilling. The AI's systematic error pattern has been internalized. The resident now makes the same error independently.
